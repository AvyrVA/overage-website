The program

Undistributed Refund Experts sends text messages about surplus funds claims to people who ask to receive them. Messages include case status updates, claim deadline reminders, requests for documents on an open claim, and replies to questions you send us.

We do not send texts to sell unrelated products, and we do not share your mobile number with anyone for their own marketing.

How you opt in

You receive texts only if you opt in, for example by ticking the optional SMS box on our case review form and giving us your mobile number. The box is unticked by default, and you can send the form without ticking it. The opt-in reads:

SMS consent, as shown on the form

I consent to receive SMS notifications & alerts from Undistributed Refund Experts at the mobile number provided, including case status updates and claim deadline reminders. Message frequency varies. Message & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe, or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms. Consent is not a condition of purchase or of receiving a case review.

After opting in you may receive a confirmation such as: “Undistributed Refund Experts: you’re subscribed to case updates. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to unsubscribe.”

Opting out

You can cancel at any time. Text STOP to the number that is messaging you. We will send one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and then no further texts. To join again, opt in the way you did the first time, or text START.

Frequency, rates and carriers

Message frequency varies with the activity on your claim. Message and data rates may apply for messages we send you and messages you send us. Contact your wireless provider about your plan.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. You must be at least 18 years old to take part.

Your privacy

Mobile numbers and opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Important disclosures

Undistributed Refund Experts is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. We identify surplus funds in the public record, assemble the documentation a claim requires, and coordinate the claim. Legal advice and legal filings are provided by a licensed attorney that Undistributed Refund Experts selects and retains. We advance that attorney’s fee, and it is reimbursed out of any amount recovered before our percentage is calculated. The attorney’s professional duties of confidentiality, loyalty and independent judgment run to you, and we do not direct the attorney’s handling of your matter. Nothing on this website creates an attorney-client relationship.

Undistributed Refund Experts is not associated with the government, and our service is not approved by the government, any county or court, or your former lender. We do not buy property, claims, or any interest in them.

We do not collect any fee before a recovery. There are no upfront fees of any kind. If nothing is recovered, you owe nothing. If funds are recovered, third-party costs are reimbursed first and our fee is never more than 30% of what remains, as stated in dollars in the agreement before you sign.

Dollar figures on this website are illustrative. They are not a quote, an estimate of your recovery, or a representation that any amount is available in your matter.
