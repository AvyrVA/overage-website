The program

{{NAME}} sends text messages about surplus funds claims to people who ask to receive them. Messages include case status updates, claim deadline reminders, requests for documents on an open claim, and replies to questions you send us.

We do not send texts to sell unrelated products, and we do not share your mobile number with anyone for their own marketing.

How you opt in

You receive texts only if you opt in, for example by ticking the optional SMS box on our case review form and giving us your mobile number. The box is unticked by default, and you can send the form without ticking it. The opt-in reads:

After opting in you may receive a confirmation such as: “{{NAME}}: you’re subscribed to case updates. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to unsubscribe.”

Opting out

You can cancel at any time. Text STOP to the number that is messaging you. We will send one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and then no further texts. To join again, opt in the way you did the first time, or text START.

Frequency, rates and carriers

Message frequency varies with the activity on your claim. Message and data rates may apply for messages we send you and messages you send us. Contact your wireless provider about your plan.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. You must be at least 18 years old to take part.

Your privacy

Mobile numbers and opt-in consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
