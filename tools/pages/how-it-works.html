How it works

Four steps, from the first letter to the day the funds are released. What happens at each one, how long it takes, and what it costs you, which is nothing until there is a recovery.

Step 1

We contact you, or you contact us.

If a letter or text from us is how you found this page: we’ll tell you exactly which property and which sale we’re calling about, and where in the public record we found it. If you came here on your own, tell us the address and we’ll look.

Either way, you can check what we tell you. Sale results and the funds held after them are public records, and the county or district clerk will confirm them for you directly.

What signing actually commits you to

Four things in the agreement you should know before you sign, not after.

Not a law firm

{{NAME}} is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. Legal advice and filings in your matter are provided by a licensed attorney that we select and retain. We advance that attorney’s fee, and it is reimbursed out of any amount recovered before our percentage is calculated. The attorney’s duties of confidentiality, loyalty and independent judgment run to you, and we do not direct the attorney’s handling of your matter.

  1. You’re appointing us as your exclusive agent on this claim.

    While the agreement is in force, we are the only ones working the claim. You aren’t filing it yourself and you aren’t hiring anyone else to.

  2. If you do pursue the claim on your own after signing,

    the agreement treats that as circumventing us, and the full percentage becomes payable immediately. We’d rather you understood that now than discovered it later.

  3. We select the attorney.

    Under the agreement, counsel on the claim is chosen by us, not by you. The attorney’s duties still run to you — confidentiality, loyalty, independent judgment — and we don’t direct their handling of your matter.

  4. Two deadlines run against you after signing.

    You have three business days to return documents we request, and three business days to confirm or object to the proposed distribution once funds arrive. If you don’t respond in that second window, the agreement lets distribution proceed without you. Both are short — tell us up front if travel, health, or anything else would make three days hard to meet.
